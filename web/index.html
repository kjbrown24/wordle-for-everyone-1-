    ? How to play

    1. Press New game.
    2. Type a 5-letter word, or tap the letters. Press Enter or Submit.
    3. Each letter gets a mark:
      • ✓ right letter, right spot
      • ~ in the word, but in a different spot
      • ✗ not in the word
    4. You have 6 guesses. If a word is not real, it does not count.
    5. Stuck? Press Hint. Hints never cost a guess.
    6. Want to stop? Press I give up and you'll see the word.
    7. Prefer to talk? Press 🎤 Say a word and say your guess, or spell it one letter at a time. The word appears on the board first, so you can check it. Then press Submit or say "submit".
    8. You can also say "hint", "I give up", "clear", or "new game".
    9. Change text size and colors with ⚙ Settings at the top.
